Custom Manufacturing

Not An Upholsterer. Not An Overlanding Shop.

Full Throttle is a custom manufacturing workshop — vehicle upholstery, canvas fabrication, overlanding gear, protective covers and furniture upholstery, all produced in-house around what the customer actually needs.

Every product starts with a measurement, not a shelf. Heavy-duty materials, industrial stitching and attention to fitment are the constants across every category we build in.
