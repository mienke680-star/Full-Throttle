Our Story

Practical, Durable, Custom.

Full Throttle Overlanding specialises in practical, durable and custom material solutions designed around what the customer actually needs — not what happens to be sitting on a shelf. Based in Industria, South Africa, the workshop takes on custom vehicle covers, protective covers, canvas work, seat covers, automotive and motorcycle upholstery, and bespoke projects that don't fit a standard mould.

Every vehicle, every rider and every piece of equipment is different — so every product is built to match, not the other way around.
